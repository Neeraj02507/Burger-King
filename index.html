About Burger King

Welcome to Burger King, the home of flame-grilled perfection! Since its founding in 1954, Burger King has been a global leader in serving delicious, high-quality burgers. Our signature Whopper and a variety of mouth-watering meals bring bold flavors and fresh ingredients to every bite.

At Burger King, we believe in "Have It Your Way", allowing you to customize your burger just the way you like it. From crispy fries to refreshing beverages, we bring a satisfying dining experience with every meal.

Join us for a taste of royalty and experience the best in fast food innovation with a side of 3D effects and interactive visuals on our website!

Our Menu

Cheese Burger

You can't go wrong with our Cheeseburger,a signature flame-grilled beef patty topped with a simple layer of melted American cheese,crinkle cut pickles,yellow mustard,and ketchup on a toasted sesame seed bun.

Crispy Veg Burger

Burger King India offers a "Crispy Veg Burger" as part of its 100% vegetarian menu, which includes a masaledar veg patty, onion, and their signature tomato herby sauce.

Whopper

The Whopper is the signature hamburger of the international fast-food restaurant chain Burger King, known for its flame-grilled beef patty, sesame seed bun, and various toppings like mayonnaise, lettuce, tomato, pickles, ketchup, and sliced onion.
